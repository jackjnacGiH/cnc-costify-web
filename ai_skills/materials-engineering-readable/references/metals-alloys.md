## Carbon Steels

### AISI 1018 (Low Carbon Steel)
**Classification**: Low carbon steel, mild steel  
**UNS**: G10180

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 440 MPa (64 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 370 MPa (54 ksi)
- Elongation: 15-20%
- Hardness: 126 HB
- Elastic Modulus: 205 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.87 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1425-1510°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 51.9 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 11.7 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent weldability
- Good machinability
- Case-hardenable (carburizing)
- Low cost
- Cannot be heat treated for higher strength

**Typical Applications**: Structural components, shafts, gears, pins, machinery parts

### AISI 1045 (Medium Carbon Steel)
**Classification**: Medium carbon steel  
**UNS**: G10450

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 570-700 MPa (as-rolled), up to 900 MPa (heat treated)
- Yield Strength: 310-450 MPa
- Elongation: 12-16%
- Hardness: 163-217 HB (normalized), up to 55 HRC (hardened)
- Elastic Modulus: 205 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.85 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1400-1480°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 49.8 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 11.5 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Heat treatable (through hardening)
- Good balance of strength and ductility
- Fair machinability
- Good wear resistance when hardened
- Moderate weldability (preheating recommended)

**Typical Applications**: Crankshafts, axles, bolts, studs, gears

### AISI 4140 (Chromium-Molybdenum Alloy Steel)
**Classification**: Low-alloy steel  
**UNS**: G41400  
**Composition**: 0.40% C, 0.95% Cr, 0.20% Mo

**Mechanical Properties** (Q&T condition):
- Tensile Strength: 850-1000 MPa
- Yield Strength: 650-850 MPa
- Elongation: 15-20%
- Hardness: 230-280 HB (annealed), 28-32 HRC (normalized), up to 54 HRC (hardened)
- Elastic Modulus: 205 GPa
- Fatigue Strength: 425-525 MPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.85 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1416°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 42.6 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 12.3 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent hardenability and toughness
- Good fatigue resistance
- Moderate machinability
- Fair weldability (requires preheating and post-weld heat treatment)
- Deep hardening capability

**Typical Applications**: Aircraft landing gear, power transmission gears, shafts, tooling, pressure vessels

## Stainless Steels

### 304 Stainless Steel (18-8)
**Classification**: Austenitic stainless steel  
**UNS**: S30400  
**Composition**: 18% Cr, 8% Ni, <0.08% C

**Mechanical Properties** (annealed):
- Tensile Strength: 515 MPa (75 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 205 MPa (30 ksi)
- Elongation: 40-50%
- Hardness: 70-80 HRB (150-180 HB)
- Elastic Modulus: 193 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.00 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1400-1450°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 16.2 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 17.3 μm/m·K
- Electrical Resistivity: 720 nΩ·m

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent corrosion resistance
- Non-magnetic in annealed condition
- Cannot be hardened by heat treatment
- Work hardens readily
- Excellent formability and weldability
- Good cryogenic properties

**Corrosion Resistance**: Excellent in atmospheric conditions, good in many chemicals, moderate in chloride environments

**Typical Applications**: Food processing equipment, kitchen equipment, chemical tanks, architectural trim

### 316 Stainless Steel (Marine Grade)
**Classification**: Austenitic stainless steel  
**UNS**: S31600  
**Composition**: 16-18% Cr, 10-14% Ni, 2-3% Mo, <0.08% C

**Mechanical Properties** (annealed):
- Tensile Strength: 515-620 MPa
- Yield Strength: 205-275 MPa
- Elongation: 35-45%
- Hardness: 79 HRB (170 HB)
- Elastic Modulus: 193 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.00 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1375-1400°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 16.3 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 15.9 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Superior corrosion resistance vs 304 (especially chlorides, acids)
- Molybdenum addition provides pitting resistance
- Non-magnetic in annealed condition
- Excellent weldability
- Higher cost than 304

**Corrosion Resistance**: Excellent in marine environments, chloride solutions, acidic conditions

**Typical Applications**: Marine equipment, chemical processing, pharmaceutical equipment, medical implants, coastal architectural applications

### 17-4 PH Stainless Steel
**Classification**: Precipitation hardening martensitic stainless steel  
**UNS**: S17400  
**Composition**: 17% Cr, 4% Ni, 4% Cu, 0.3% Nb

**Mechanical Properties** (H900 condition):
- Tensile Strength: 1310 MPa (190 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 1210 MPa (175 ksi)
- Elongation: 10%
- Hardness: 40-47 HRC
- Elastic Modulus: 196 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.80 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1400-1440°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 17.9 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 10.8 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Heat treatable to very high strength levels
- Good corrosion resistance (not as good as 304/316)
- Magnetic
- Good toughness and ductility at high strength levels
- Excellent fatigue properties

**Typical Applications**: Aerospace components, valve parts, chemical processing, pump shafts, oil field equipment

## Tool Steels

### D2 Tool Steel (High-Carbon, High-Chromium)
**Classification**: Cold work tool steel  
**UNS**: T30402  
**Composition**: 1.5% C, 12% Cr, 1% Mo, 1% V

**Mechanical Properties** (hardened and tempered):
- Hardness: 54-61 HRC (typical 58-60 HRC)
- Tensile Strength: 1790-2070 MPa
- Yield Strength: 1380-1650 MPa
- Elastic Modulus: 210 GPa
- Impact Toughness: 10-25 J (relatively brittle)

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.70 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1421°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 20 W/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent wear resistance
- High compressive strength
- Good dimensional stability in heat treatment
- Deep hardening capability
- Air hardening (doesn't require oil quench)
- Moderate toughness
- Fair machinability in annealed condition

**Typical Applications**: Cutting tools, punches, dies, knives, shear blades, forming rolls

### A2 Tool Steel (Air-Hardening)
**Classification**: Cold work tool steel  
**UNS**: T30102  
**Composition**: 1% C, 5% Cr, 1% Mo, 0.2% V

**Mechanical Properties** (hardened and tempered):
- Hardness: 57-62 HRC
- Tensile Strength: 1650-1930 MPa
- Impact Toughness: 20-35 J (better than D2)

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 7.86 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1427°C

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent balance of toughness and wear resistance
- Good dimensional stability
- Air hardening (minimal distortion)
- Better toughness than D2
- Good machinability
- Moderate wear resistance

**Typical Applications**: Blanking dies, forming dies, shear blades, punches, gauges

## Aluminum Alloys

### 6061-T6 Aluminum
**Classification**: Heat-treatable aluminum-magnesium-silicon alloy  
**Temper**: T6 (solution heat treated and artificially aged)

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 310 MPa (45 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 276 MPa (40 ksi)
- Elongation: 12-17%
- Hardness: 95 HB
- Elastic Modulus: 69 GPa
- Fatigue Strength: 96.5 MPa (at 500M cycles)

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 2.70 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 582-652°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 167 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 23.6 μm/m·K
- Electrical Conductivity: 43% IACS

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent corrosion resistance
- Good weldability
- Good formability
- Easily machined
- Good strength-to-weight ratio
- Responds well to anodizing
- Most versatile heat-treatable aluminum alloy

**Typical Applications**: Aircraft fittings, marine fittings, bike frames, automotive parts, structural components, furniture

### 7075-T6 Aluminum
**Classification**: Heat-treatable aluminum-zinc alloy  
**Temper**: T6 (solution heat treated and artificially aged)  
**Composition**: 5.6% Zn, 2.5% Mg, 1.6% Cu, 0.2% Cr

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 572 MPa (83 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 503 MPa (73 ksi)
- Elongation: 11%
- Hardness: 150 HB
- Elastic Modulus: 72 GPa
- Fatigue Strength: 159 MPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 2.81 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 477-635°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 130 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 23.4 μm/m·K
- Electrical Conductivity: 33% IACS

**Characteristics**:
- One of the strongest aluminum alloys
- Good fatigue strength
- Average machinability
- Poor weldability (prone to cracking)
- Moderate corrosion resistance (lower than 6061)
- High strength-to-weight ratio
- Stress corrosion cracking susceptibility

**Typical Applications**: Aircraft structures, aerospace components, high-stress parts, rock climbing equipment, bicycle components

### 2024-T3 Aluminum
**Classification**: Heat-treatable aluminum-copper alloy  
**Temper**: T3 (solution heat treated, cold worked, naturally aged)  
**Composition**: 4.4% Cu, 1.5% Mg, 0.6% Mn

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 483 MPa (70 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 345 MPa (50 ksi)
- Elongation: 18%
- Hardness: 120 HB
- Elastic Modulus: 73 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 2.78 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 502-638°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 121 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 22.9 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent strength-to-weight ratio
- Good fatigue resistance
- Poor corrosion resistance (requires cladding or coating)
- Poor weldability
- Good machinability
- Widely used in aerospace

**Typical Applications**: Aircraft fuselage, wings, tension members, truck wheels, rivets

## Titanium Alloys

### Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5)
**Classification**: Alpha-beta titanium alloy  
**UNS**: R56400  
**Composition**: 6% Al, 4% V, balance Ti

**Mechanical Properties** (annealed):
- Tensile Strength: 895-930 MPa (130-135 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 828-862 MPa (120-125 ksi)
- Elongation: 10-14%
- Hardness: 30-38 HRC
- Elastic Modulus: 114 GPa
- Fatigue Strength: 510 MPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 4.43 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1604-1660°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 6.7 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 8.6 μm/m·K
- Electrical Resistivity: 1780 nΩ·m

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent strength-to-weight ratio
- Excellent corrosion resistance
- Biocompatible
- Good elevated temperature properties (up to 400°C)
- Difficult to machine (requires special tools and techniques)
- Expensive
- Poor thermal conductivity

**Typical Applications**: Aerospace components, medical implants, marine hardware, high-performance automotive parts, sports equipment

### CP Titanium Grade 2
**Classification**: Commercially pure titanium  
**UNS**: R50400

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 345 MPa (50 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 275 MPa (40 ksi)
- Elongation: 20%
- Hardness: 80-90 HRB
- Elastic Modulus: 103 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 4.51 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1670°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 16 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 8.6 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent corrosion resistance (best of titanium grades)
- Good formability
- Excellent weldability
- Lower strength than Ti-6Al-4V
- Better ductility than alloys
- Biocompatible

**Typical Applications**: Chemical processing equipment, heat exchangers, medical devices, marine components

## Copper Alloys

### C260 Brass (Cartridge Brass, 70/30)
**Classification**: Copper-zinc alloy  
**Composition**: 70% Cu, 30% Zn

**Mechanical Properties** (annealed):
- Tensile Strength: 340 MPa (49 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 105 MPa (15 ksi)
- Elongation: 65%
- Hardness: 55 HRB
- Elastic Modulus: 110 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.53 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 940°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 120 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 20.5 μm/m·K
- Electrical Conductivity: 28% IACS

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent cold working properties
- Good corrosion resistance
- Easy to machine
- Good for deep drawing
- Cannot be hardened by heat treatment
- Dezincification can occur in some environments

**Typical Applications**: Ammunition casings, plumbing fixtures, radiator cores, springs, fasteners

### C932 Bearing Bronze (SAE 660)
**Classification**: Tin bronze, bearing bronze  
**Composition**: 83% Cu, 7% Sn, 7% Pb, 3% Zn

**Mechanical Properties** (as-cast):
- Tensile Strength: 241 MPa (35 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 124 MPa (18 ksi)
- Elongation: 18%
- Hardness: 60 HB
- Elastic Modulus: 100 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.93 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 855-1010°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 59 W/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent bearing and wear properties
- Good machinability (lead content)
- Good corrosion resistance
- Self-lubricating properties
- Pressure-tight castings
- Lead provides chip breaking

**Typical Applications**: Bearings, bushings, gears, worm gears, valve components

## Nickel Alloys

### Inconel 718
**Classification**: Nickel-chromium superalloy  
**UNS**: N07718  
**Composition**: 50-55% Ni, 17-21% Cr, 4.75-5.5% Nb+Ta, 2.8-3.3% Mo, 0.65-1.15% Ti, 0.2-0.8% Al

**Mechanical Properties** (aged condition, room temperature):
- Tensile Strength: 1375 MPa (200 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 1100 MPa (160 ksi)
- Elongation: 20%
- Hardness: 40 HRC
- Elastic Modulus: 200 GPa

**High Temperature Properties** (at 650°C):
- Tensile Strength: 1035 MPa
- Yield Strength: 965 MPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.19 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1260-1336°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 11.4 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 13.0 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent high-temperature strength (up to 700°C)
- Precipitation hardenable
- Excellent oxidation and corrosion resistance
- Good weldability for a superalloy
- Difficult to machine
- Excellent fatigue and creep resistance
- Very expensive

**Typical Applications**: Gas turbine engines, rocket engines, nuclear reactors, oil and gas drilling equipment, aerospace fasteners

### Monel 400
**Classification**: Nickel-copper alloy  
**UNS**: N04400  
**Composition**: 63% Ni, 28-34% Cu, 2.5% Fe, 2% Mn

**Mechanical Properties** (annealed):
- Tensile Strength: 550 MPa (80 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 240 MPa (35 ksi)
- Elongation: 40%
- Hardness: 65-85 HRB
- Elastic Modulus: 179 GPa

**Physical Properties**:
- Density: 8.80 g/cm³
- Melting Point: 1300-1350°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 21.8 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 13.9 μm/m·K
- Electrical Resistivity: 547 nΩ·m

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent corrosion resistance in seawater and acids
- Good mechanical properties from cryogenic to elevated temperatures
- Resistant to stress-corrosion cracking
- Non-magnetic
- Moderate machinability
- Good weldability

**Typical Applications**: Marine engineering, chemical processing, oil refining, valves, pumps, shafts, heat exchangers
