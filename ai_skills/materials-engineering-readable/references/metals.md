## 1. Carbon & Alloy Steels

### AISI 1018 (Mild Steel)
- **Density:** 7.87 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 440 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 370 MPa
- **Elongation:** 15%
- **Hardness:** 126 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 200 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 1480–1530°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 51.9 W/m·K
- **CTE:** 11.7 μm/m·°C
- **Standard:** ASTM A108 / AISI 1018
- **Applications:** Shafts, pins, gears, fasteners, general machining
- **Machinability:** Excellent (rating 78%)
- **Weldability:** Excellent
- **Limitations:** Low corrosion resistance, not suitable for high-temp

### AISI 1045 (Medium Carbon Steel)
- **Density:** 7.85 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 570 MPa (normalized)
- **Yield Strength:** 310 MPa
- **Elongation:** 16%
- **Hardness:** 163 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 200 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 1475°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 49.8 W/m·K
- **Standard:** ASTM A29 / AISI 1045
- **Applications:** Axles, crankshafts, gears, rolls, connecting rods
- **Machinability:** Good (rating 57%)
- **Weldability:** Fair (preheat required)
- **Heat Treatment:** Can be hardened to 54–60 HRC

### AISI 4140 (Chromoly Steel)
- **Density:** 7.85 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 655–1020 MPa (depending on temper)
- **Yield Strength:** 415–855 MPa
- **Elongation:** 17–25%
- **Hardness:** 197–302 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 205 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 1416°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 42.6 W/m·K
- **CTE:** 12.3 μm/m·°C
- **Standard:** ASTM A322 / AISI 4140
- **Applications:** Gears, shafts, bolts, aircraft parts, high-strength fasteners
- **Machinability:** Good (rating 66%)
- **Weldability:** Good (low-hydrogen electrode)
- **Heat Treatment:** Oil quench, temper to desired hardness

### AISI 4340 (High-Strength Alloy Steel)
- **Density:** 7.85 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 745–1670 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 470–1570 MPa
- **Elongation:** 10–22%
- **Hardness:** 217–578 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 205 GPa
- **Standard:** ASTM A322 / AISI 4340
- **Applications:** Aircraft landing gear, crankshafts, high-stress structural parts
- **Notes:** Superior toughness vs 4140; used in critical aerospace/defense

## 2. Stainless Steels

### AISI 304 (18-8 Stainless)
- **Density:** 8.00 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 515 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 205 MPa
- **Elongation:** 40%
- **Hardness:** 92 HRB / 201 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 193 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 1399–1454°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 16.2 W/m·K
- **CTE:** 17.2 μm/m·°C
- **Standard:** ASTM A240 / EN 1.4301
- **Applications:** Food processing equipment, kitchen equipment, chemical containers
- **Machinability:** Fair (rating 45%)
- **Weldability:** Excellent
- **Limitations:** Susceptible to chloride stress corrosion cracking
- **Compliance:** RoHS compliant; food-grade certified

### AISI 316 (Marine Grade Stainless)
- **Density:** 7.99 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 580 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 290 MPa
- **Elongation:** 40%
- **Hardness:** 95 HRB
- **Young's Modulus:** 193 GPa
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 16.3 W/m·K
- **Standard:** ASTM A240 / EN 1.4401
- **Applications:** Marine hardware, pharmaceutical equipment, chemical processing
- **Key Advantage over 304:** Contains 2–3% Mo for superior chloride resistance
- **Compliance:** USP Class VI, food-grade, medical-grade available

### AISI 316L (Low Carbon 316)
- **Density:** 7.99 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 485 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 170 MPa
- **Standard:** ASTM A240 / EN 1.4404
- **Key Advantage:** Max 0.03% C — prevents sensitization during welding
- **Applications:** Heavy-gauge welded components, medical implants

### AISI 420 (Martensitic Stainless)
- **Density:** 7.73 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 586 MPa (annealed) / up to 1900 MPa (hardened)
- **Hardness:** Up to 50 HRC (hardened)
- **Standard:** ASTM A276
- **Applications:** Cutlery, surgical instruments, valve parts, molds
- **Notes:** Hardenable; moderate corrosion resistance only

### AISI 17-4 PH (Precipitation Hardened)
- **Density:** 7.78 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 930–1310 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 724–1170 MPa
- **Elongation:** 10–14%
- **Hardness:** 28–44 HRC
- **Standard:** ASTM A564 / UNS S17400
- **Applications:** Aerospace, nuclear, chemical, food — where high strength + corrosion resistance needed
- **Key Advantage:** Aged at low temp — minimal distortion vs conventional quench-harden

## 3. Tool Steels

### D2 (Cold Work Tool Steel)
- **Density:** 7.70 g/cm³
- **Hardness (hardened):** 60–64 HRC
- **Tensile Strength:** 758 MPa (annealed)
- **Standard:** ASTM A681 / DIN 1.2379
- **Applications:** Blanking dies, forming dies, punches, shear blades
- **Key Properties:** High wear resistance, good dimensional stability

### H13 (Hot Work Tool Steel)
- **Density:** 7.76 g/cm³
- **Hardness (hardened):** 48–52 HRC
- **Standard:** ASTM A681 / DIN 1.2344
- **Applications:** Die casting dies (aluminum, zinc), hot forging dies, extrusion tooling
- **Key Properties:** Excellent thermal fatigue resistance, hot hardness

### P20 (Plastic Mold Steel)
- **Density:** 7.85 g/cm³
- **Hardness:** 28–34 HRC (pre-hardened)
- **Standard:** DIN 1.2311
- **Applications:** Injection mold bases, compression molds, plastic tooling
- **Key Advantage:** Pre-hardened — no heat treatment after machining

## 4. Aluminum Alloys

### 6061-T6
- **Density:** 2.70 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 310 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 276 MPa
- **Elongation:** 12%
- **Hardness:** 95 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 68.9 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 582–652°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 167 W/m·K
- **CTE:** 23.6 μm/m·°C
- **Electrical Conductivity:** 43% IACS
- **Standard:** ASTM B209 / ASTM B221
- **Applications:** Structural frames, bicycle frames, automotive parts, marine fittings
- **Machinability:** Excellent (rating 150%)
- **Weldability:** Good
- **Anodizing:** Excellent response

### 7075-T6
- **Density:** 2.81 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 572 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 503 MPa
- **Elongation:** 11%
- **Hardness:** 150 HB
- **Young's Modulus:** 71.7 GPa
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 130 W/m·K
- **Standard:** ASTM B209
- **Applications:** Aircraft structures, high-stress parts, sporting goods
- **Notes:** Highest strength common aluminum alloy; poor weldability; stress corrosion risk

### 2024-T3
- **Density:** 2.78 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 483 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 345 MPa
- **Elongation:** 18%
- **Standard:** ASTM B209
- **Applications:** Aircraft fuselage, wings, structural members
- **Notes:** High fatigue resistance; must be anodized/clad — bare surface corrodes

### 5052-H32
- **Density:** 2.68 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 228 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 193 MPa
- **Elongation:** 12%
- **Standard:** ASTM B209
- **Applications:** Sheet metal, marine, fuel tanks, pressure vessels
- **Key Advantage:** Best corrosion resistance among common alloys; excellent formability

### 6063-T5
- **Density:** 2.70 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 186 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 145 MPa
- **Standard:** ASTM B221
- **Applications:** Architectural extrusions, window frames, door frames
- **Key Advantage:** Excellent surface finish after anodizing; lower strength than 6061

### A380 (Die Cast)
- **Density:** 2.71 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 325 MPa
- **Hardness:** 80 HB
- **Standard:** ASTM B85
- **Applications:** Automotive housings, power tools, electronics housings
- **Key Advantage:** Most widely used aluminum die cast alloy; excellent fluidity

## 5. Copper & Copper Alloys

### C11000 (Electrolytic Tough Pitch Copper)
- **Density:** 8.94 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 220 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 69 MPa
- **Elongation:** 45%
- **Hardness:** 40 HRF
- **Electrical Conductivity:** 101% IACS
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 391 W/m·K
- **Standard:** ASTM B152
- **Applications:** Electrical wire, bus bars, heat exchangers, PCB
- **Notes:** Highest conductivity copper; 99.9% Cu minimum

### C26000 (Cartridge Brass, 70/30)
- **Density:** 8.53 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 338–896 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 103–448 MPa
- **Elongation:** 8–66%
- **Electrical Conductivity:** 28% IACS
- **Standard:** ASTM B19
- **Applications:** Ammunition casings, radiator cores, hardware, fasteners
- **Notes:** Best cold-working brass; excellent deep-drawing capability

### C36000 (Free-Cutting Brass)
- **Density:** 8.49 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 338 MPa
- **Hardness:** 78 HRB
- **Machinability:** 100% (reference standard for machinability)
- **Standard:** ASTM B16
- **Applications:** Screw machine products, valves, fittings, gears
- **Notes:** Best machinability of all copper alloys; contains 3% Pb

### C93200 (Bearing Bronze / SAE 660)
- **Density:** 8.93 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 241 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 124 MPa
- **Hardness:** 60 HRB
- **Standard:** ASTM B584 / SAE 660
- **Applications:** Bearings, bushings, pump impellers, wear plates
- **Notes:** Self-lubricating properties; most popular bronze bearing alloy

## 6. Titanium Alloys

### Grade 2 (Commercially Pure Ti)
- **Density:** 4.51 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 345 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 275 MPa
- **Elongation:** 20%
- **Standard:** ASTM B265
- **Applications:** Chemical processing, heat exchangers, marine, medical implants
- **Notes:** Excellent corrosion resistance; biocompatible; lower strength than alloys

### Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) — Most Common Ti Alloy
- **Density:** 4.43 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 950 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 880 MPa
- **Elongation:** 14%
- **Hardness:** 36 HRC
- **Young's Modulus:** 113.8 GPa
- **Melting Point:** 1604–1660°C
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 7.2 W/m·K
- **CTE:** 8.6 μm/m·°C
- **Standard:** ASTM B265 Grade 5 / AMS 4928
- **Applications:** Aerospace structures, jet engine parts, biomedical implants, high-performance automotive
- **Machinability:** Poor (rating ~22%) — requires carbide tooling, low speeds, flood coolant
- **Notes:** ~60% lighter than steel at similar strength; ITAR controlled in some forms

## 7. Nickel Alloys (Superalloys)

### Inconel 625
- **Density:** 8.44 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 930 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 517 MPa
- **Elongation:** 42.5%
- **Max Service Temp:** 980°C
- **Standard:** ASTM B443
- **Applications:** Aerospace, marine, chemical processing, nuclear
- **Key Properties:** Excellent oxidation + corrosion resistance at high temps

### Inconel 718
- **Density:** 8.22 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 1240–1430 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 1034–1172 MPa
- **Max Service Temp:** 704°C (continuous)
- **Standard:** AMS 5663 / ASTM B637
- **Applications:** Jet engines, rocket motors, gas turbines, cryogenic tanks
- **Notes:** Most widely used superalloy; difficult to machine

### Hastelloy C-276
- **Density:** 8.89 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 786 MPa
- **Applications:** Chemical processing, flue gas desulfurization, waste treatment
- **Key Properties:** Outstanding resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion

## 8. Cast Irons

### Gray Iron (ASTM A48 Class 30)
- **Density:** 7.15 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 214 MPa
- **Compressive Strength:** 572 MPa (excellent)
- **Hardness:** 179–229 HB
- **Machinability:** Excellent
- **Applications:** Engine blocks, cylinder heads, machine tool bases, pipes
- **Notes:** Excellent vibration damping; brittle in tension

### Ductile Iron (ASTM A536 Grade 65-45-12)
- **Density:** 7.10 g/cm³
- **Tensile Strength:** 448 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 310 MPa
- **Elongation:** 12%
- **Hardness:** 143–187 HB
- **Applications:** Crankshafts, gears, pipe fittings, heavy-duty valves
- **Key Advantage over Gray Iron:** Nodular graphite → much higher toughness and ductility

## 9. Substitution Guide (Metals)

| Original | Substitute | Reason | Key Difference | Process Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AISI 304 SS | AISI 316 SS | Better Cl⁻ resistance | Higher cost (+15%) | None |
| AISI 316 SS | Duplex 2205 | Higher strength + corrosion | Harder to form | Adjust forming parameters |
| 6061-T6 Al | 7075-T6 Al | Need higher strength | Lower weldability | Use fasteners instead of welding |
| 6061-T6 Al | 5052-H32 Al | Need better formability | Lower strength | OK for sheet metal |
| Ti-6Al-4V | 17-4 PH SS | Cost reduction | 2× heavier | Check load requirements |
| Steel 1018 | Al 6061 | Weight reduction | 1/3 weight, 1/3 strength | Redesign cross-section |
| Inconel 625 | 316L SS | Cost reduction | Lower temp limit (450°C) | Verify temperature |
| C36000 Brass | Al 2011 | Weight reduction | Better machinability | Nearly identical process |
| Gray Iron | Ductile Iron | Need ductility/toughness | Similar cost (+10%) | Same casting process |
