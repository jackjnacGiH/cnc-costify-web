## Commodity Thermoplastics

### Polyethylene (PE)

#### HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)
**Classification**: Semi-crystalline thermoplastic  
**Density**: 0.95-0.97 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 20-30 MPa
- Yield Strength: 26-33 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 10-1200% (grade dependent)
- Flexural Modulus: 800-1400 MPa
- Hardness: 60-70 Shore D
- Impact Strength (Izod): 20-215 J/m (notched)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 130-137°C
- Glass Transition (Tg): -125°C
- Max Service Temperature: 90-110°C (continuous)
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.42-0.51 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 100-200 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent chemical resistance
- Good impact strength, especially at low temperatures
- Good moisture resistance
- Low friction coefficient
- Easy to process (injection molding, extrusion, blow molding)
- Good electrical insulation
- UV degradation (requires stabilizers)
- Low temperature brittleness above certain molecular weights

**Typical Applications**: Bottles, containers, pipes, toys, household goods, cutting boards, chemical tanks

#### LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene)
**Density**: 0.91-0.93 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 8-25 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 100-650%
- Flexural Modulus: 150-300 MPa
- Hardness: 40-50 Shore D

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 105-115°C
- Max Service Temperature: 70-90°C

**Characteristics**:
- More flexible than HDPE
- Excellent chemical resistance
- Good impact resistance
- Excellent moisture barrier
- Good processability
- Lower strength and stiffness than HDPE

**Typical Applications**: Film, bags, squeeze bottles, wire insulation, flexible tubing

#### UHMWPE (Ultra-High Molecular Weight Polyethylene)
**Density**: 0.93-0.94 g/cm³  
**Molecular Weight**: 3-6 million g/mol

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 20-45 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 350-525%
- Flexural Modulus: 500-800 MPa
- Abrasion Resistance: Exceptional (15x better than carbon steel)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 130-136°C
- Max Service Temperature: 80-100°C

**Characteristics**:
- Exceptional wear and abrasion resistance
- Extremely low friction coefficient
- Excellent impact strength
- Self-lubricating
- Chemical inert
- Difficult to process (sintering, ram extrusion)
- Cannot be melt processed by conventional methods
- Expensive

**Typical Applications**: Bearings, gears, wear strips, chute liners, medical implants (joint replacements), bulletproof vests

### Polypropylene (PP)
**Classification**: Semi-crystalline thermoplastic  
**Density**: 0.90-0.91 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 30-40 MPa
- Yield Strength: 25-40 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 100-600%
- Flexural Modulus: 1200-1700 MPa
- Hardness: 70-85 Shore D
- Impact Strength (Izod): 20-80 J/m (notched, varies greatly with temperature)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 160-165°C
- Glass Transition (Tg): -10 to 0°C
- Max Service Temperature: 90-120°C
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 100-105°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.22 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 80-100 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Good chemical resistance (acids, alkalis, organic solvents)
- Excellent fatigue resistance (living hinge applications)
- Good heat resistance
- Low density (floats in water)
- Good processability
- Susceptible to UV degradation (requires stabilizers)
- Poor low-temperature impact resistance
- Lower tensile strength than PE

**Typical Applications**: Automotive parts, living hinges, containers, packaging, fibers, textiles, medical devices, laboratory equipment

### Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)

#### Rigid PVC (uPVC - Unplasticized)
**Density**: 1.38-1.55 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 40-60 MPa
- Yield Strength: 45-55 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 20-40%
- Flexural Modulus: 2400-4100 MPa
- Hardness: 75-85 Shore D
- Impact Strength: Low (brittle at room temperature)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 80-85°C
- Max Service Temperature: 60-70°C
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 65-75°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.16 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 50-100 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Good chemical resistance
- Excellent flame resistance (self-extinguishing)
- Good weatherability
- Rigid and hard
- Low cost
- Good electrical insulation
- Brittle at low temperatures
- Degrades at high temperatures (releases HCl)

**Typical Applications**: Pipes, fittings, window frames, siding, credit cards, electrical conduit

#### Flexible PVC (Plasticized)
**Density**: 1.10-1.35 g/cm³ (varies with plasticizer content)

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 10-25 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 200-450%
- Hardness: 50-90 Shore A (depends on plasticizer)

**Characteristics**:
- Flexible and soft
- Good abrasion resistance
- Good electrical insulation
- Plasticizer migration can occur

**Typical Applications**: Wire insulation, flooring, inflatable products, blood bags, tubing

### ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene)
**Classification**: Amorphous thermoplastic terpolymer  
**Density**: 1.04-1.07 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 40-55 MPa
- Yield Strength: 38-48 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 3-75% (grade dependent)
- Flexural Modulus: 2000-2700 MPa
- Hardness: 105-120 Rockwell R
- Impact Strength (Izod): 215-400 J/m (notched)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 105°C
- Max Service Temperature: 70-90°C
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 85-105°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.17-0.25 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 90-140 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Good impact resistance
- Good dimensional stability
- Excellent processability (injection molding, extrusion)
- Easy to paint and plate
- Moderate chemical resistance
- Fair UV resistance (requires stabilizers)
- Good balance of properties
- Opaque appearance

**Typical Applications**: Automotive trim, appliance housings, toys (LEGO), electronics enclosures, 3D printing (FDM), pipes

## Engineering Thermoplastics

### Nylon (Polyamide - PA)

#### PA6 (Nylon 6)
**Classification**: Semi-crystalline engineering thermoplastic  
**Density**: 1.12-1.14 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties** (dry, 23°C):
- Tensile Strength: 70-85 MPa
- Yield Strength: 50-90 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 30-100%
- Flexural Modulus: 2000-3200 MPa
- Hardness: 70-80 Shore D
- Impact Strength (Izod): 50-100 J/m (notched)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 220°C
- Glass Transition (Tg): 50-60°C
- Max Service Temperature: 80-150°C (short term)
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 65-75°C (dry), 45-55°C (conditioned)
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.25 W/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent wear and abrasion resistance
- Good chemical resistance to oils, greases, hydrocarbons
- Low friction coefficient
- Hygroscopic (absorbs moisture, affecting properties)
- Good fatigue resistance
- Easily molded and machined
- Poor resistance to strong acids and oxidizing agents
- UV degradation (requires stabilizers)

**Property Changes with Moisture**:
- Dry PA6 is stiff and brittle
- Moisture-conditioned PA6 is tougher but weaker (tensile strength drops ~50%)
- Impact strength improves significantly with moisture

**Typical Applications**: Gears, bearings, bushings, rollers, fasteners, cable ties, automotive components

#### PA66 (Nylon 66)
**Density**: 1.13-1.15 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties** (dry, 23°C):
- Tensile Strength: 75-95 MPa
- Yield Strength: 55-85 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 15-80%
- Flexural Modulus: 2200-3400 MPa
- Impact Strength: Higher than PA6

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 260-265°C
- Glass Transition (Tg): 50-60°C
- Max Service Temperature: 90-120°C (continuous)
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 75-90°C (dry)

**Characteristics**:
- Higher melting point than PA6
- Better heat resistance than PA6
- Higher tensile strength and stiffness
- More expensive than PA6
- Also hygroscopic
- Better fatigue resistance than PA6

**Typical Applications**: High-performance gears, connectors, automotive under-hood components, industrial parts

#### PA12 (Nylon 12)
**Density**: 1.01-1.02 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 50-60 MPa
- Flexural Modulus: 1200-1500 MPa

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 178-180°C
- Max Service Temperature: 80-100°C

**Characteristics**:
- Low moisture absorption (much less than PA6/PA66)
- Better dimensional stability in humid conditions
- Excellent chemical resistance
- Good low-temperature impact strength
- More flexible than PA6/PA66
- Lower strength than PA6/PA66
- More expensive

**Typical Applications**: Fuel lines, pneumatic tubing, cable jacketing, sports equipment

### Polycarbonate (PC)
**Classification**: Amorphous engineering thermoplastic  
**Density**: 1.20-1.22 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 55-75 MPa
- Yield Strength: 60-70 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 80-150%
- Flexural Modulus: 2200-2400 MPa
- Hardness: 115-125 Rockwell M
- Impact Strength (Izod): 600-850 J/m (notched) - Exceptional!

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 145-150°C
- Max Service Temperature: 115-130°C
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 130-140°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.19-0.22 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 65-70 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Outstanding impact resistance (virtually unbreakable)
- Excellent optical clarity (transparent)
- Good heat resistance
- Good dimensional stability
- Flame resistant (self-extinguishing)
- UV stabilized grades available
- Susceptible to scratching
- Attacked by solvents, alkalies, aromatic hydrocarbons
- Stress cracking susceptibility
- Notch sensitive

**Typical Applications**: Safety glasses, visors, riot shields, light covers, electrical enclosures, CDs/DVDs, automotive headlamp lenses, medical devices

### POM / Acetal (Polyoxymethylene)

#### Acetal Homopolymer (Delrin®)
**Density**: 1.41-1.42 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 65-70 MPa
- Yield Strength: 60-70 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 25-75%
- Flexural Modulus: 2600-3100 MPa
- Hardness: 120 Rockwell M
- Impact Strength (Izod): 80-120 J/m

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 175-178°C
- Max Service Temperature: 90-104°C
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 0.46 MPa): 110-124°C
- Thermal Expansion: 110 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent dimensional stability
- Very low moisture absorption
- Excellent fatigue resistance
- Low friction coefficient
- Good chemical resistance (except strong acids/bases)
- Easy to machine
- Good creep resistance
- Slightly higher stiffness and strength than copolymer
- Poorer thermal stability than copolymer

**Typical Applications**: Precision parts, gears, bearings, bushings, pump components, valves, zippers

#### Acetal Copolymer
**Density**: 1.40-1.41 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 61-68 MPa (slightly lower than homopolymer)
- Flexural Modulus: 2400-2900 MPa

**Characteristics**:
- Better thermal stability than homopolymer
- Better chemical resistance than homopolymer
- Improved center-line porosity in thick sections
- Better hot water and alkali resistance
- Similar overall properties to homopolymer

**Typical Applications**: Similar to homopolymer, preferred for chemical and hot water exposure

## High-Performance Thermoplastics

### PEEK (Polyether Ether Ketone)
**Classification**: Semi-crystalline high-performance thermoplastic  
**Density**: 1.30-1.32 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 90-100 MPa
- Yield Strength: 90-100 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 20-50%
- Flexural Modulus: 3600-4000 MPa
- Hardness: 100 Rockwell M
- Impact Strength (Izod): 80-85 J/m (notched)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 343°C
- Glass Transition (Tg): 143°C
- Max Service Temperature: 250°C (continuous), 310°C (short term)
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 1.8 MPa): 160°C
- Thermal Conductivity: 0.25 W/m·K
- Thermal Expansion: 47 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Exceptional high-temperature performance
- Excellent chemical resistance (resistant to most chemicals)
- Excellent wear resistance
- Low moisture absorption
- Good radiation resistance (gamma sterilizable)
- Inherently flame resistant (UL94 V-0)
- Biocompatible
- Excellent mechanical properties retained at high temp
- Very expensive
- Difficult to process (high processing temperatures)

**Typical Applications**: Aerospace components, oil & gas seals, medical implants, semiconductor equipment, high-performance bearings, electrical insulators

### PEI / Ultem (Polyetherimide)
**Classification**: Amorphous high-performance thermoplastic  
**Density**: 1.27 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 105 MPa
- Yield Strength: 110 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 60%
- Flexural Modulus: 3300 MPa
- Impact Strength (Izod): 53 J/m (notched)

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 217°C
- Max Service Temperature: 170°C (continuous), 200°C (short term)
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 1.8 MPa): 200°C
- Thermal Expansion: 56 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent high-temperature performance
- Inherently flame resistant (UL94 V-0)
- Excellent dimensional stability
- Transparent amber color
- Good chemical resistance
- High strength and stiffness
- Low smoke generation
- Sterilizable (autoclave, ETO, gamma)
- Attacked by strong bases and some solvents
- Expensive (but less than PEEK)

**Typical Applications**: Aircraft interior components, medical devices, automotive under-hood parts, food service, electrical connectors

### PTFE / Teflon (Polytetrafluoroethylene)
**Classification**: Semi-crystalline fluoropolymer  
**Density**: 2.13-2.20 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 20-35 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 200-400%
- Flexural Modulus: 400-600 MPa
- Hardness: 50-65 Shore D
- Compressive Strength: 10-13 MPa

**Thermal Properties**:
- Melting Point: 327°C
- Max Service Temperature: 260°C (continuous), 290°C (short term)
- Low Temperature Limit: -270°C
- Thermal Expansion: 135 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Lowest coefficient of friction of any solid (0.05-0.10)
- Outstanding chemical resistance (inert to nearly all chemicals)
- Excellent high and low temperature performance
- Excellent electrical insulation
- Non-stick surface
- Low mechanical strength
- High cold flow (creep) under load
- Cannot be melt processed (sintered instead)
- Expensive
- Non-reactive (difficult to bond)

**Typical Applications**: Seals, gaskets, bearings, chemical processing equipment, non-stick coatings, wire insulation, labware

### PSU (Polysulfone)
**Density**: 1.24 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 70 MPa
- Flexural Modulus: 2600 MPa

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 185°C
- Max Service Temperature: 150°C (continuous)
- Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 1.8 MPa): 174°C

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent high-temperature performance
- Transparent (amber)
- Excellent hydrolytic stability
- Sterilizable
- Good toughness
- Attacked by polar solvents, ketones

**Typical Applications**: Medical devices, food processing equipment, plumbing fittings, automotive

## Thermosets

### Epoxy Resins
**Classification**: Thermosetting polymer  
**Density**: 1.10-1.40 g/cm³ (cured)

**Mechanical Properties** (typical casting resin):
- Tensile Strength: 55-130 MPa
- Flexural Modulus: 3000-4500 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 1-6%
- Compressive Strength: 100-200 MPa

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): 110-250°C (formulation dependent)
- Max Service Temperature: 120-220°C
- Thermal Expansion: 45-65 μm/m·K

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent adhesion to various substrates
- High strength and stiffness
- Good chemical resistance
- Excellent electrical insulation
- Low shrinkage during cure
- Brittle (low impact strength)
- Two-part system (resin + hardener)
- Cannot be remolded or reformed
- Good elevated temperature properties

**Typical Applications**: Adhesives, coatings, composite matrix, electronic encapsulation, tooling, flooring

### Phenolic Resins
**Density**: 1.25-1.30 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 35-60 MPa
- Flexural Modulus: 7000-10000 MPa
- Compressive Strength: 140-280 MPa

**Thermal Properties**:
- Max Service Temperature: 150-200°C
- Excellent flame resistance (does not support combustion)

**Characteristics**:
- High heat resistance
- Excellent electrical insulation
- Good chemical resistance
- Brittle
- Relatively inexpensive
- Char forming (doesn't burn)
- Dark color (brown/black)

**Typical Applications**: Electrical components, circuit breakers, brake pads, adhesives, laminates

### Polyurethane (PU) Thermosets
**Density**: 1.05-1.25 g/cm³

**Characteristics**:
- Wide range of hardness (Shore A to Shore D)
- Excellent abrasion resistance
- Good chemical resistance
- Good flexibility
- Can be foamed or solid
- Two-part system

**Typical Applications**: Coatings, adhesives, sealants, foam (rigid and flexible), wheels, rollers, seals

## Elastomers

### Natural Rubber (NR)
**Density**: 0.92-0.93 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 20-30 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 700-800%
- Hardness: 40-100 Shore A
- Tear Strength: Excellent

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): -70°C
- Max Service Temperature: 80°C

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent resilience and rebound
- Good tear and fatigue resistance
- Good low-temperature flexibility
- Poor oil and ozone resistance
- Biodegradable
- Attacked by oxidation, UV, ozone

**Typical Applications**: Tires, seals, hoses, vibration dampers, rubber bands

### Silicone Rubber (VMQ)
**Density**: 1.10-1.60 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 4-10 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 100-800%
- Hardness: 10-90 Shore A
- Tear Strength: Fair to good

**Thermal Properties**:
- Glass Transition (Tg): -100 to -50°C
- Max Service Temperature: 200-300°C (continuous)
- Low Temperature Limit: -100°C

**Characteristics**:
- Exceptional temperature range (-100 to +300°C)
- Excellent weathering and ozone resistance
- Good chemical resistance
- Physiologically inert (biocompatible)
- Excellent electrical insulation
- Low mechanical strength
- Expensive
- Poor oil and fuel resistance

**Typical Applications**: Medical devices, seals for high/low temp, food-grade applications, electrical insulation

### Nitrile Rubber (NBR, Buna-N)
**Density**: 1.00 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 10-25 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 300-600%
- Hardness: 30-95 Shore A

**Thermal Properties**:
- Max Service Temperature: 100-120°C

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent oil and fuel resistance
- Good abrasion resistance
- Fair to good chemical resistance
- Poor ozone and weather resistance
- Acrylonitrile content determines oil resistance (higher = better)

**Typical Applications**: Oil seals, O-rings, fuel hoses, gaskets, automotive applications

### EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)
**Density**: 0.86 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 10-20 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 300-600%
- Hardness: 30-90 Shore A

**Thermal Properties**:
- Max Service Temperature: 120-150°C
- Low Temperature Limit: -50°C

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent weather, ozone, UV resistance
- Good heat and aging resistance
- Excellent steam resistance
- Good water and chemical resistance
- Poor oil and fuel resistance
- Low cost

**Typical Applications**: Automotive weather stripping, roofing membranes, electrical insulation, hoses for coolant

### Fluoroelastomer (FKM, Viton®)
**Density**: 1.80-1.85 g/cm³

**Mechanical Properties**:
- Tensile Strength: 10-20 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 150-300%
- Hardness: 60-95 Shore A

**Thermal Properties**:
- Max Service Temperature: 200-230°C (continuous), 300°C (short term)
- Low Temperature Limit: -20 to -40°C (grade dependent)

**Characteristics**:
- Excellent chemical resistance (acids, fuels, oils)
- Excellent high-temperature performance
- Excellent oxidation resistance
- Low gas permeability
- Poor low-temperature flexibility
- Very expensive

**Typical Applications**: Aerospace O-rings, automotive fuel system seals, chemical processing seals, oil & gas applications

## Material Selection Quick Reference

### By Temperature Resistance (Continuous Service):
1. PTFE (260°C)
2. PEEK (250°C)
3. PEI/Ultem (170°C)
4. PSU (150°C)
5. PC (115-130°C)
6. PA66 (90-120°C)
7. PP (90-120°C)
8. ABS (70-90°C)
9. HDPE (90-110°C)

### By Impact Strength (Best to Worst):
1. Polycarbonate (PC) - Exceptional
2. ABS - Very Good
3. Polyamides (Nylon) - Good to Very Good
4. HDPE - Good
5. PP - Fair (poor at low temp)
6. Acetal - Good
7. PEEK - Good
8. Rigid PVC - Poor (brittle)

### By Chemical Resistance (General):
1. PTFE - Inert to nearly all chemicals
2. PEEK - Excellent
3. PP - Very Good
4. HDPE - Very Good
5. PVC - Good
6. Polyamides - Good (except strong acids/bases)
7. ABS - Fair to Good
8. PC - Fair (attacked by many solvents)

### By Cost (Relative, $/kg):
1. PP, HDPE, PVC ($)
2. LDPE, ABS ($$)
3. Nylon (PA6/PA66) ($$-$$$)
4. PC, Acetal ($$$)
5. PEI/Ultem ($$$$)
6. PEEK, PTFE ($$$$$+)

### By Wear Resistance:
1. UHMWPE - Exceptional
2. PEEK - Excellent
3. Acetal (POM) - Excellent
4. Nylon (PA6/PA66) - Excellent
5. PTFE - Good (low friction but wears)
6. HDPE - Good
7. PC - Fair
8. ABS - Fair

### By Moisture Absorption (Dry to Wet):
1. PTFE, PP, PE - Virtually none
2. Acetal, PC, ABS - Very low (<0.3%)
3. PEI/Ultem - Low (0.25%)
4. PEEK - Low (0.5%)
5. PA12 - Moderate (1.5%)
6. PA6, PA66 - High (8-9%) - Properties significantly affected!
